About Philip Juma Simiyu

An Information Technology professional passionate about solving technical problems, supporting users, and making technology work better for people and organizations.

WHO I AM

Driven by Technology and Problem-Solving

My name is Philip Juma Simiyu. I have a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from the University of Kabianga and I am pursuing a Diploma in Technical Trainer–CBET, specializing in Computer Studies and Mathematics at the Kenya School of Technical Vocational Education and Training (KSTVET), Nairobi.

My professional background includes ICT support, computer hardware and software troubleshooting, system maintenance, networking, technical training, and data management. These experiences have helped me develop a practical approach to identifying problems, assisting users, and supporting reliable technology services.

I value professionalism, teamwork, clear communication, continuous learning, and responsible use of technology. I aim to contribute to environments where dependable IT systems help people perform their work effectively.
